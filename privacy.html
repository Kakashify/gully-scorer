Gully Scorer

Privacy Policy

Effective 4 November 2026

In short
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your matches, team names and player names stay on your phone. We never receive them.
  • The microphone is used only while you have voice scoring switched on. Offline voice is processed on your phone and is never recorded or sent anywhere.
  • We don't sell or share any personal information, because we don't collect any.

Who we are

Gully Scorer is a cricket scoring app published by Kakashify. It runs from kakashify.github.io/gully-scorer and is also available as an Android app. This policy covers both.

Information stored on your phone

To keep your score while you play, the app saves the following in your phone's storage for the app:

  • Match details: team names, overs, rules you chose, every ball scored, and results.
  • Player names, if you choose to track players.
  • Your settings, such as the voice and announcer options.

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else by the app. You can delete it at any time with Past matches → Clear, by abandoning a match in progress, by clearing the app's storage in your phone settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Microphone and voice scoring

Gully Scorer asks for microphone access only when you tap the Voice button. Voice scoring stops when you tap it again or leave the app.

  • Offline voice (the default). Speech is turned into scores entirely on your phone. Audio is not recorded, not saved and not sent anywhere. The voice packs this needs are downloaded once from our website and kept on your phone.
  • Google voice (optional). If you choose Google voice in the menu, your browser sends what the microphone hears while voice scoring is on to Google's speech service to convert it to text. That processing is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

Announcer

The announcer reads scores and player names aloud using your phone's built-in text-to-speech voice.

Sharing scorecards

The Send to WhatsApp button opens WhatsApp with the scorecard text ready to send. Nothing is shared until you send it yourself in WhatsApp, and WhatsApp's own privacy policy then applies. The Copy button only copies the scorecard to your phone's clipboard.

Website hosting

The app's files are served by GitHub Pages. As with any website, your phone's IP address is sent when it downloads the app's files. GitHub states that when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor's IP address is logged and stored for security purposes (see GitHub's documentation and the GitHub Privacy Statement). We don't receive these logs. The app's fonts and voice engine are bundled with the app, so it doesn't load anything from other websites.

Cookies, ads and analytics

Gully Scorer doesn't use cookies, advertising, analytics or any tracking tools.

Children

Gully Scorer is a general-audience app. It doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we'll update this page and change the effective date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer email address shown on the Gully Scorer listing on Google Play.
